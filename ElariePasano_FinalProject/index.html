YOUR ADOPTION JOURNEY

Give a paw a forever home.

Everything you need to know before adopting a pet.

01

Choose a pet

Browse the available animals and find a pet that suits your lifestyle and living situation.

02

Prepare your requirements

Prepare a valid ID, household information, and details about your experience with pets.

03

Submit an application

Contact the animal's rescuer or shelter and complete their adoption application.

04

Meet your future pet

Arrange a meeting and complete any required interview or screening process.

05

Prepare your home

Prepare food, water, bedding, toys, and a safe environment for your new companion.

06

Welcome them home

Once approved, welcome your pet and provide lifelong love, attention, and veterinary care.

Before you adopt

  • Make sure everyone in your household agrees.
  • Be financially prepared for food and veterinary expenses.
  • Provide a safe and comfortable living environment.
  • Be ready for a long-term commitment.
  • Never adopt an animal on impulse.

Actual adoption requirements may vary depending on the shelter or rescuer.

What to do when you find a stray animal
  1. Observe the animal from a safe distance.
  2. Avoid chasing or frightening the animal.
  3. Offer clean water if it is safe to do so.
  4. Record its general location and condition.
  5. Contact a verified rescuer or veterinarian.
  6. Do not give medication without professional advice.

Avoid handling frightened or injured animals unless it is safe.

04 / LEARN & CARE

Care better, love better.

Simple, responsible care helps pets thrive.

Everyday essentials
